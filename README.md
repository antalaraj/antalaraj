## About

I’m pursuing a **PGDM in AI & Data Science at Adani Institute of Digital Technology Management (AIDTM)**. I enjoy taking ideas from problem discovery to working prototypes, combining Python, machine learning, computer vision, and Generative AI.

My recent work includes AI-assisted healthcare imaging, multi-agent grant research, document-grounded meeting analysis, and AI-powered applications.

- **Focus:** AI engineering, applied machine learning, Generative AI, and data science
- **Building:** End-to-end AI applications and useful developer-friendly tools
- **Interested in:** RAG, agentic workflows, model evaluation, and deployment
